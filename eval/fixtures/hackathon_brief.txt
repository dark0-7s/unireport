HackAI 2026: National Student Innovation Hackathon
Date: November 2-3, 2026
Location: University Innovation Center, Hall B
Department: School of Informatics and Computing

Tracks & Problem Statements:
1. HealthTech: Automated Medical Imaging Triage
2. FinTech: Decentralized Micro-Insurance Contracts
3. ClimateAI: Urban Heat Island Mitigation via Sensor Telemetry

Awards & Leaderboard:
- 1st Place ($5,000): Team NeuroPulse for "Realtime EEG Seizure Predictor"
- 2nd Place ($2,500): Team CarbonTrace for "Campus Emissions Audit Bot"
- Best Innovation ($1,000): Team QuantFlow for "Low-Power Edge Tensor Processor"

Sponsors: Google Cloud (Platinum, $10,000), AWS for Education (Gold, $5,000), GitHub Campus (Partner).
Turnout: 340 hackers across 85 teams. 12 finalist teams selected for live jury demos. Devpost archive: https://devpost.com/hackai-2026.
